Precision: of the bookings flagged as likely no-shows, how many were. Recall: of the real no-shows, how many were caught. F1 balances both, which matters because attended and no-show bookings are not balanced. ROC-AUC measures how well the predicted probabilities rank no-shows above attenders (0.5 = random, 1.0 = perfect).
