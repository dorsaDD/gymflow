Members are grouped by how they actually use the gym with K-Means clustering on an RFM model (Recency, Frequency, Monetary) plus tenure and reliability. The number of groups is chosen automatically by the silhouette score. Administrators can target offers at a segment with the rule fact member.segment, for example a win-back offer for "At risk" members.
