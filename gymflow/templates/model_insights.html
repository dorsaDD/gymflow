Features used

FeatureMeaning

Algorithm comparison

Two different model families are trained on the same 75% split and evaluated on the same held-out 25% — the one with the better F1 score (tie-broken by accuracy) is the one actually used for live predictions on the dashboard and rosters.

AlgorithmAccuracyPrecisionRecallF1

Precision: of the bookings flagged as likely no-shows, how many actually were. Recall: of the bookings that actually ended in a no-show, how many were caught. F1 balances the two — it's what decides the winner here rather than accuracy alone, since with an uneven mix of attended/no-show rows a model can get high accuracy just by predicting the majority class every time.
