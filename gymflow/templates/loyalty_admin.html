Supported rule format

Every condition is combined with AND. Use only whitelisted facts and operators. Example: 10 points on every attended class:

{"conditions": [], "action": {"type": "award_points", "points": 10}}

Example: award 50 points once a member has attended at least 8 classes:

{"conditions": [{"fact": "member.attended_classes", "op": "gte", "value": 8}], "action": {"type": "award_points", "points": 50}}

Conditions: member.age, member.tier, member.active_days, member.attended_classes, member.attended_classes_month, member.attended_classes_30d, member.spending_total, member.spending_total_including_invoice, member.purchase_count, member.days_since_first_purchase, invoice.amount. Operators: eq, ne, gt, gte, lt, lte, in. Actions: point awards, percentage/fixed invoice discount, or grant_reward with a configured reward ID. Class categories, location, and membership-type facts are unavailable because this project does not store them.

Administrator point adjustment

Manual corrections change spendable balance, not lifetime qualifying points or tier. Every adjustment is recorded in the member ledger.
